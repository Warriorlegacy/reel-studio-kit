# Public Shorts comparison · 2026-11-15

## Method

Collected public Shorts listing metadata only, without account credentials or downloading videos. Sampled the latest 20 public entries for Fireship, ByteByteGo, and CodeWithHarry, plus the 4 available public entries for Cadet SINGH. Harkirat Singh returned no usable listing and was excluded. View counts change over time; this is a directional snapshot, not a forecast.

The yt-viral swipe tool ranked each title by views divided by that channel's sample median. Its formula label matches title wording only; it does not establish why the video performed well. Full sample: youtube-shorts-sample.json.

## What to borrow for Signhify

- Among the relevant tech outliers, titles name a recognizable system or consequence (Big Tech production, Netflix, Redis, APIs) and make one small promise or open question. The title comparison cannot prove this caused the views.
- The Hindi channel's sampled AI outlier frames the topic through a human conflict. For this pivot, translate that energy into a real software failure, not controversy bait.
- The Cadet SINGH outlier is a music-status post and should not be treated as evidence for Hindi tech packaging. The channel has only four sampled public entries, so its baseline is especially noisy; treat the next 5–10 Hindi-tech uploads as the start of a new series baseline.
- The current pilot follows this direction: two customers, one slot; then one clear system mechanism and payoff.
- After publishing, compare first-second retention, chose-to-view, average view duration, percentage viewed, and comment/save/share response against the channel's own new baseline.

## Limits

The sample is small, the channels serve different audiences, and public view counts change. One title-only formula match is a descriptive label, not causal evidence. Do not interpret these ratios as a guarantee of future reach.
