YouTube recommendation controls (on eligible Home / Watch Next items):

1. Open the ⋮ menu and choose **Not interested** for a video.
2. Open the ⋮ menu and choose **Don’t recommend channel** for a channel.
3. To review watch/search history, open YouTube History in your Google Account settings.

These are feedback and history controls; they are not a promise of an instant feed reset. YouTube’s official guide: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6342839?hl=en
