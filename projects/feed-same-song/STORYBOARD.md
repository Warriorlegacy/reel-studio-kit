# Storyboard · Feed recommendation bridge

**Purpose:** a Hindi-tech bridge for viewers familiar with music/status Shorts. Explain examples of YouTube recommendation signals without claiming a fixed ranking formula.

**Canvas:** 1080×1920, 30 fps. Use the reel kit’s warm paper, ink, terracotta, and green identity. Devanagari display text; Inter for English UI labels; JetBrains Mono for small technical labels. Keep all text inside the Reel Studio Kit safe box.

| Approx. time | Narration beat | Motion-graphics direction | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | “YouTube feed में वही Shorts बार-बार क्यों दिखते हैं?” | Two warm-paper feed cards enter already showing the same topic, then separate into distinct recommendations. A terracotta question mark lands beside the repeating card; no logo intro. | **कौन चुनता?** |
| 3–8s | Each viewer can see different recommendations. | Two phones split across the frame. A watch-history tile drops into each; different tile order makes each feed diverge. | **हर viewer की feed अलग** |
| 8–13s | Watch history; what you watched. | A timeline scrubber moves past a watched card. A receipt-like “WATCH HISTORY” strip folds into the feed tray. | **WATCH HISTORY** |
| 13–17s | Search history; what you searched. | Search icon expands into a query card; the query travels to the same tray. Keep the exact query generic, not user-specific. | **SEARCH HISTORY** |
| 17–23s | Subscriptions and likes/dislikes. | Three small icon cards—subscribe, like, dislike—join the tray. Cards move at different speeds; never show numerical weights. | **SUBSCRIBE · LIKE / DISLIKE** |
| 23–29s | Multiple signals; one view is not the whole feed. | A single “1 VIEW” remote button presses but changes nothing alone. Several signal cards feed into a branching paper mechanism and produce two different feeds. | **एक view पूरी feed नहीं चलाता** |
| 29–36s | Use feedback on eligible Home/Watch Next items. | Show a clearly labeled Home card with a three-dot menu. Open it, then highlight “Not interested” and “Don’t recommend channel.” A small “FEEDBACK” stamp lands; do not animate a total feed wipe. | **FEEDBACK · instant reset नहीं** |
| 36–41s | Comment FEED; read the pinned guide. | A comment bubble lands beside a pinned note card. Its top line reads “YouTube controls.” Rotate the phone card back into the opening position to create a clean loop. | **Comment FEED** |

## Motion and sound

- Give the opening a hard visual question in frame zero; do not begin with a logo.
- Add a meaningful movement or state change about every two seconds: card slide, fold, stamp, search lens, menu open, or feed swap.
- Make the largest composition turns at the watch/search transition, the multi-signal reveal, and the Home feedback control.
- Use one restrained tap sound for each signal card, then one soft paper-fold sound as the cards enter the feed tray. Keep music low under the narration.
- Never display signal percentages, multipliers, guaranteed outcomes, or an “algorithm score.”
- Loop the opening status card from the final pinned-comment shot.
