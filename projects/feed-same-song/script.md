## Twist

YouTube documents multiple personalized recommendation signals, and says different features can rely on different signals. A single view or like is not a complete explanation of a viewer’s feed. The control menu provides feedback that may tune future recommendations; do not claim it instantly resets a feed.

## Facts and sources

- YouTube says recommendations are personalized and describes viewer history, search history, subscriptions, likes/dislikes, “Not interested,” “Don’t recommend channel,” and satisfaction feedback as signals. Different features can rely on signals differently: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387?hl=en
- The Shorts feed is personalized; YouTube considers whether viewers choose to watch or ignore a recommendation, average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes, and survey responses: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?hl=en
- YouTube says “Not interested” and “Don’t recommend channel” feedback may be used to tune recommendations. The controls appear on eligible Home/Watch Next surfaces: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6342839?hl=en
- The statement “one view does not control your whole feed” is a plain-language inference from the documented use of multiple signals; it is not a disclosed ranking formula.
- The video does not claim that one Bhojpuri status guarantees a specific recommendation, does not assign weights to signals, and does not promise an instant feed reset.
